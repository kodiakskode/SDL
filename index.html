How this is put together

Nothing leaves the browser. Every page is a static file. Lead status, notes and your light/dark choice live in this browser's localStorage, so they are per-device and not shared between machines. The Pool Finder has Back up / Restore buttons to move that tracking across.

Leads come from two compiled lists: 750 NSW-registered architects taken from NSW Architects Registration Board public profiles, and 750 NSW landscape and garden design businesses from public listings. Only 28 of the landscape rows carry separate public LDI / AILDM evidence — those are tagged, the rest are marked unverified rather than claimed as members. Phone coverage on that list is near total; email coverage is not, because most of those businesses publish a contact form instead.

Pool Finder is the tool as built, carried across whole — same data, same scoring, same filters — with the address-copying added and the styling wired into this dashboard's theme.
