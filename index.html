About the data

Leads — 750 NSW-registered architects from NSW Architects Registration Board profiles, and 750 NSW landscape and garden design businesses from public listings. Only 28 of the landscape rows carry separate LDI / AILDM evidence; the rest are marked unverified. Phone coverage there is near total, email coverage is not.

Pool Finder — the tool as built, carried across whole. Same data, same scoring, same filters, with address copying added.

Storage — lead status, notes and your light/dark choice live in this browser's localStorage, so they are per-device. The Pool Finder's Back up / Restore buttons move that tracking between machines.

SDL Creations · everything runs in your browser. Lead tracking, notes and the theme are stored on this device only.
Leads from public professional listings · pool data OpenStreetMap (ODbL) and NSW Spatial Services (CC BY 4.0).
